## ⚡ Executive Summary

Fintech Analyst and AI Product Builder operating at the intersection of **Credit Risk Modeling**, **Lending Analytics**, and **Applied Machine Learning Workflows**. Focused on bringing engineering discipline to finance by turning financial statements, bank ledgers, and credit covenants into automated underwriting and decision infrastructure.

- 🏦 **Specialization:** Credit underwriting, lending analytics, borrower risk segmentation, DSCR / liquidity stress-testing.
- 🚀 **Flagship Platform:** **[Decoded Deals & Synthesis](https://deal-decoding.vercel.app/)** — Real-time automated financial document deconstruction, term sheet analysis, and covenant tracking.
- 🏛️ **Venture Framework:** **[Bodhidharman](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bodhidharman/)** — Building practical systems that combine AI + Data + Finance + Automation.
- 🛡️ **Operational Track Record:** Ex-Jumio Corporation — scaled AML/KYC identity verification pipelines, anti-fraud evaluation, and curated ML datasets with ~99% accuracy benchmarks.
